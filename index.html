Eric Jollès

I'm a doctoral student at EPFL, working broadly on network security and privacy topics. I am fortunate to be advised by Carmela Troncoso in the SPRING lab, where I design and attack existing systems to create new solutions that make the Internet a more private place.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Security Engineer at Open Systems AG and completed a cybersecurity internship at armasuisse's Cyber Defense Campus. I hold a joint Master's in Cyber Security from EPFL and ETH Zurich.

In my free time, I enjoy flying planes, biking, hiking, and skiing.
